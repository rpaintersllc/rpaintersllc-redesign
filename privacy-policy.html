Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

R Painters LLC (“R Painters,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit rpaintersllc.com, request an estimate, contact us, or submit information through our advertisements and lead forms on platforms such as Facebook and Instagram.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including your name, email address, telephone number, property or service address, the type of service requested, desired project timing, project details or photographs, your preferred contact method, and information contained in an estimate or quote you voluntarily ask us to review.

Our website and service providers may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as device type, browser type, IP address, referring page, general website activity, and advertising or analytics identifiers.

Please do not send Social Security numbers, financial-account credentials, medical information, or other sensitive personal information through our website or advertising forms.

How We Use Information

We may use information to respond to questions and estimate requests; review your painting project; schedule estimates and services; communicate by telephone, text message, email, Messenger, Instagram, or WhatsApp; prepare proposals; provide requested services and customer support; maintain business records; improve our website, advertising, and customer experience; prevent fraud or misuse; and comply with applicable legal requirements.

Submitting a request does not obligate you to purchase services.

Facebook and Instagram Lead Forms

When you submit an Instant Form or communicate with us through Facebook or Instagram, Meta may provide R Painters LLC with the information you submitted. Meta separately processes information according to its own terms and privacy policy. We use lead-form information to respond to your request, discuss your project, provide an estimate, and follow up about the services you requested.

Calls, Text Messages, and Electronic Communications

If you provide a telephone number or other contact information, you authorize R Painters LLC to contact you about your inquiry or project. Message and data rates may apply. Consent to receive marketing text messages is not a condition of purchasing services. You may opt out of marketing text messages by replying STOP. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us directly.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that help us operate our business, including website hosting, customer-management, scheduling, communications, advertising, analytics, and payment providers. These providers receive information only as reasonably necessary to perform services for us.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law or a lawful request; protect our customers, business, property, or legal rights; investigate suspected fraud, abuse, or security incidents; or complete a business transfer subject to appropriate protections.

Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics and advertising measurement tools, to maintain functionality, understand website usage, measure advertising performance, and improve our services. You can adjust cookie permissions through your browser. Disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, provide services, maintain business and tax records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. Information no longer reasonably required will be deleted, anonymized, or securely disposed of when practical.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information. However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may contact us to ask what personal information we maintain about you, correct inaccurate contact information, request deletion subject to legal and business-record requirements, withdraw a marketing preference, or ask questions about this policy. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are intended for adults seeking painting and related property services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

External Platforms and Links

Our website may link to third-party platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, Yelp, Google, WhatsApp, and scheduling or estimate services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our practices or legal requirements change. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
