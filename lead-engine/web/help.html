How this works

1. Receive customer requests

A visitor fills out the estimate form, passes the security check and agrees to be contacted. Their request is stored in Supabase, and a generic alert goes to rpaintersinsc@gmail.com. The email links to your private dashboard, where the customer details are available.

The private preview is for you. It requires your ChatGPT account, followed by your R Painters application login. Do not promote this private preview to homeowners.

2. Follow up and track the job

  1. Open the alert or sign into your dashboard using rpaintersinsc@gmail.com and your application password.
  2. Choose Customer requests. Call or email the customer using the details shown. The system does not contact customers automatically.
  3. Move the status through New → Contacted → Visit scheduled → Quoted → Won, or Lost when appropriate.
  4. Keep estimates, contracts, scheduling and payments in DripJobs. This MVP does not synchronize with DripJobs.

Use the Status filter to focus on new or unquoted requests. Refresh loads up to 200 recent records. Ignore records labeled TEST; they are not real customers.

3. Review public opportunities

Choose Public opportunities. The official-source links help you find current notices. Check whether painting is in scope, whether the deadline is still open, and whether you meet the requirements. Save the original publication URL, scope and notes. Mark the record Relevant or Dismissed after review.

This MVP supports manual research and tracking. It does not automatically scrape Facebook, Nextdoor, homeowner data or government portals, and it does not submit bids or send messages for you.

4. Understand email alerts

Pending means queued or waiting for retry. Processing means a worker has temporarily claimed the alert. Accepted means the email service accepted it; it does not independently prove inbox delivery. Failed means the retry limit or a permanent error needs attention.

New requests trigger an immediate attempt. A protected scheduled check runs every five minutes when configured. You can also use Retry next pending alert. The queue retries at most eight times and stops after twelve hours. A failed email never deletes the customer request. Check the dashboard if an expected alert is missing.

5. Where promotion fits

The form converts visitors into requests; it does not generate visitors automatically. After the public website connection is approved and tested, share the public estimate URL on your Google Business Profile, Facebook and Instagram profiles, relevant neighborhood posts where allowed, email signature, business cards and QR codes. Never share your administrator login or private dashboard as the customer link.

Start with your existing audience and free placements. Use a different source tag for each placement, for example ?utm_source=instagram&utm_campaign=free_estimate. The request stores that attribution so you can see which placements bring inquiries.

Do not buy advertising until the public request flow and email alerts have passed a live test. The prepared production candidate is /request-estimate-lead-engine.html; it is not live on rpaintersllc.com until the release is approved.

Privacy and limits

Only the authorized administrator can read or update requests. Keys that grant server access never belong in the website. Customer details remain in your dashboard rather than in notification emails. Photo uploads, automated opportunity discovery, automatic customer replies, contact verification and DripJobs synchronization are outside this MVP.
