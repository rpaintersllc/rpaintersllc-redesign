How to use discovery

Connect homeowner request searches

Sign in with your R Painters application account. The dashboard starts on Discovered opportunities. In Connect homeowner request searches, follow the Google Alerts steps and add the RSS feed URLs to your dashboard. Feed URLs are stored privately; they are never shown in public source code.

The Charleston bid source is connected. Homeowner discovery requires connected request feeds. A public-bid monitor alone does not provide homeowner inquiries.

Check sources and review candidates

Connected sources are checked every 15 minutes. Click Check connected sources now for a manual check. The activity panel shows source errors, last-check times and qualifying candidates. Zero matches is a valid result. If a check shows an error or becomes stale, review that source.

The worker looks for relevant services, local areas and request language. It rejects obvious contractor ads, hiring posts and filled requests. Homeowner feed entries older than 24 hours are excluded. Search feeds can index old content late, so the feed timestamp is not proof that the original request is new.

Open each original source before responding. Check the post date, current need, location and work requested. Public bids also require scope, deadline, addenda and qualification checks. Mark the record Relevant or Dismissed. Duplicate original URLs do not create new records or overwrite your review.

Respond and track your work

Use the suggested reply as a starting point and edit it for the actual request. Nothing is posted or sent to a prospect automatically. Keep proposals, scheduling, contracts and payments in DripJobs; this application does not synchronize with it.

Google Alerts coverage is incomplete and can be delayed. Facebook groups, Nextdoor and paid lead platforms are not connected. The system does not reveal hidden contacts or guarantee inquiries or jobs.

Email alerts

A new saved opportunity queues a generic alert to rpaintersinsc@gmail.com with a link to your private dashboard. Alerts do not contain prospect contact details. One queued alert is attempted immediately after a scan; remaining or interrupted alerts are checked every five minutes, with at most eight attempts over twelve hours. Retry opportunity alert triggers a manual attempt.

Accepted means the email provider accepted the message; it does not prove inbox delivery. Clearly labeled SYSTEM TEST records are synthetic and should be ignored when counting opportunities.

Promotion

This is your private research tool. You do not need to promote this dashboard to homeowners. Your existing business website remains the place for people to contact R Painters.
