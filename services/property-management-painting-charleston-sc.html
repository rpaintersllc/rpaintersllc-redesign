Clear work orders, access and scheduling

For a faster estimate, send the property address, work order or requested scope, photos, vacancy status, access instructions and target completion date. For occupied properties, confirm the tenant or site-contact process before scheduling. The proposal identifies the surfaces, preparation and coating work included so additions are not assumed.

Painting-related repairs—not unrestricted maintenance

We can include compatible preparation such as nail holes, minor drywall patches, damaged corner areas, caulking and priming when they are part of the painting scope. We do not advertise plumbing, electrical, structural repair or general handyman services. Active water sources and structural movement must be corrected before finish work.

Property-management painting FAQs

Can you work from a property-management work order?

Yes. Share the work order, address, requested surfaces, access requirements and timeline. We will review the painting scope and provide a written estimate for qualifying projects.

Can drywall repairs be included before painting?

Yes, when they are compatible with the painting project. Drywall patches, spot preparation and priming can be listed separately in the proposal after inspection.

Do you handle occupied and vacant properties?

Both may be considered. Access, tenant or site-contact coordination, working hours and the surfaces included are confirmed before scheduling.

What should I include with an estimate request?

Include the property address, vacancy status, photos, surfaces to be painted, known repairs and the requested completion window. This helps us prepare for the walkthrough and identify missing scope details early.
