Drywall repairs before painting

Painting looks best when the substrate is stable and properly prepared. We evaluate holes, cracks, nail pops, damaged seams, water-stained areas after the moisture source has been corrected, and other surface defects that can affect the finished result.

When drywall repair and painting belong in the same project, we can document both in one written scope. For related work, see our interior painting service and drywall and ceiling repair project.
