Our cabinet refinishing process

Cabinet projects typically include protection, cleaning, sanding or deglossing as appropriate, priming and application of a durable cabinet/trim coating. Door and drawer-front application methods are confirmed in the proposal.

Professional cabinet coatings

We commonly use professional-grade products selected for cabinetry and trim, including Sherwin-Williams Emerald Urethane Trim Enamel when appropriate for the project.

See our cabinet refinishing work

Our cabinet refinishing project page and portfolio show real cabinetry completed by R Painters LLC. We evaluate the existing finish, door and drawer count, preparation needs and application method before preparing the written estimate.

Why preparation matters on cabinets

Cabinet surfaces are touched and cleaned far more often than ordinary walls. Cleaning, sanding or deglossing, appropriate priming and a durable cabinet-grade finish are therefore central to the scope rather than optional extras.
