Painting between tenants

Whether a rental needs a full repaint or targeted touch-ups, we review wall condition, existing colors, trim and door finishes, access, and the move-in deadline before preparing a written estimate. Interior walls, ceilings, trim, doors and painting-related drywall patches can be included as needed; the final scope depends on the property condition.

Preparing a rental for the next occupant

We identify nail holes, scuffs, peeling coatings, staining and other surface issues during the walkthrough. Where appropriate, the painting scope can include patching, sanding, spot priming and repainting. Water intrusion, structural repairs, plumbing and electrical work are not offered as general maintenance services.

For landlords and property managers

To request a useful quote, send the property location, unit size or photos, the surfaces to be painted, whether the unit is vacant or occupied, access instructions, and the preferred completion window. We can review qualifying work orders and provide a written painting scope. For recurring or multi-unit work, see our property-management painting services.

Related painting services

Projects may also require interior painting, painting-related drywall repair, or trim and door painting, depending on the condition of the unit.
