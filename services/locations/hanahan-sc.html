House Painting Services in Hanahan

R Painters LLC provides free on-site estimates for qualifying Hanahan painting projects. We document the rooms or exterior surfaces, existing condition, preparation, colors and finish expectations in a written proposal so the work is clear before scheduling.

Interior Repaints and Occupied Homes

For an interior painting estimate, identify the walls, ceilings, trim and doors you want included. We discuss furniture protection, room access, existing stains, nail holes, sheen and color changes during the walkthrough. Occupied and vacant properties require different access and protection planning.

Drywall, Ceilings, Trim and Accent Work

Drywall patches and painting preparation can be included with the repaint after inspection. Ask separately about popcorn-ceiling removal, skim coating, trim and door painting, or accent walls and wainscoting so the correct finish level is priced.

Exterior Painting for Lowcountry Conditions

Exterior work may include siding, painted brick, wood trim, doors, shutters and porches. The estimate identifies cleaning, scraping, sanding, caulking, repairs, primer and coatings where required. Washing is available only as preparation for an exterior painting project; we do not offer standalone pressure washing.

Cabinet Painting and Refinishing

Kitchen and bathroom cabinets need a different preparation plan from wall painting. Our cabinet refinishing service can include cleaning, sanding, priming and a durable enamel system. Share photos and the approximate number of doors and drawers with your estimate request.

Rental and Property-Management Painting

Hanahan rental turns and managed-property scopes can include repainting, painting-related drywall patches, trim and doors. Provide the work order, vacancy status, access details, photos and target completion window for a clearer property-management painting estimate.

Painting Scope Boundaries

R Painters LLC handles painting and compatible surface preparation. We do not advertise plumbing, electrical, structural repair or unrestricted handyman services. Leak sources and structural movement must be corrected before drywall finishing or painting.

Serving Hanahan and Nearby Areas

Our Summerville-based team also serves North Charleston, Goose Creek, Ladson, Summerville and nearby Charleston-area communities. View completed R Painters project photos before planning your estimate.
