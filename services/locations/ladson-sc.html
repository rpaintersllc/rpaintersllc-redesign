Residential Painting Services in Ladson

R Painters LLC is an insured painting company based nearby in Summerville. We prepare written estimates for Ladson projects after reviewing the surfaces, existing condition, repairs, colors and timing. The scope identifies what will be protected, prepared, primed and painted before scheduling.

Interior Painting for Walls, Ceilings and Trim

Our interior painting service can include walls, ceilings, baseboards, crown molding, doors and frames. During the walkthrough, tell us about tall areas, occupied rooms, furniture, stains or color changes so access and preparation can be included accurately.

Exterior House Painting and Preparation

Exterior estimates can cover siding, painted brick, trim, doors, shutters, porches and other compatible surfaces. We inspect peeling coating, failed caulk, exposed areas and moisture concerns before recommending preparation. Surface washing is offered only when included with an exterior painting project—not as a standalone cleaning service.

Cabinets, Drywall and Detailed Finish Work

Ask about cabinet painting and refinishing, painting-related drywall repairs, popcorn-ceiling removal, trim and door painting, or an accent wall. Cabinet projects require cleaning, sanding, priming and a coating selected for repeated use.

Rental Turnovers and Light Commercial Painting

Vacant homes, rentals and suitable small commercial spaces may need a defined repaint or touch-up scope before occupancy. Share the address, access plan, photos, surfaces and target date when requesting rental turnover painting or light commercial painting.

Painting-Related Repairs Only

Minor drywall patches, nail holes, caulking and surface preparation may be included with painting after inspection. R Painters LLC does not advertise plumbing, electrical, structural repair or unrestricted handyman work. Active leaks and structural movement must be corrected before finish work.
