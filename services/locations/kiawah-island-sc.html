Residential painting for Kiawah Island homes

R Painters LLC is an insured painting company based in Summerville and serving Kiawah Island as part of the Charleston-area service territory. We help homeowners plan interior repaints, exterior painting, cabinet refinishing, trim and door painting, and painting-related drywall or ceiling preparation.

Interior finishes, cabinets and repairs

Interior estimates can include walls, ceilings, baseboards, crown molding, doors and frames. We identify holes, cracks, stains, failing caulk and other painting-related preparation before defining the scope. Explore interior painting, cabinet refinishing, trim and door painting and drywall repair before painting.

Exterior preparation for Lowcountry exposure

Exterior paint performance depends on preparation and choosing products for the existing siding, trim, masonry, porch or other substrate. We review peeling coatings, gaps, moisture staining and exposed areas before recommending a finish plan. Surface washing is available only when needed as part of an exterior painting project; R Painters does not offer standalone pressure washing.

Outdoor wood projects can also include deck or fence staining when the surface condition and coating system are appropriate.
