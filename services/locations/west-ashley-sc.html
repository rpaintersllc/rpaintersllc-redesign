Residential painters serving West Ashley

R Painters LLC is an insured painting company based in Summerville and serving West Ashley within the Charleston metro. We help homeowners plan interior repaints, exterior painting, cabinet refinishing, trim and door painting, accent walls, and painting-related drywall or ceiling preparation.

Each project is reviewed individually for scope, surface condition, access and timing. This service-area page does not represent a separate West Ashley office.

Interior painting, repairs and property-ready work

Interior scopes can include walls, ceilings, baseboards, crown molding, doors and frames. Before painting, we identify holes, cracks, stains, damaged drywall and failing caulk that may affect the finish. Explore interior painting, cabinet refinishing, trim and door painting, drywall repair before painting and popcorn ceiling removal.

Occupied homes, rental turnovers and legitimate light-commercial painting are scoped according to the surfaces and scheduling requirements.

Exterior preparation for Lowcountry homes

Exterior estimates consider siding, trim, masonry, porches and other painted surfaces, along with peeling coatings, gaps, stains and exposed areas. Surface washing is available only when required to prepare an exterior painting project; R Painters does not offer standalone pressure washing.

When the surface condition and coating system are appropriate, outdoor work may also include deck or fence staining.
