James Island Painting Estimate FAQs

How do I request an estimate?

Call 843-475-9927 or request a free estimate online. Include the project address, surfaces, photos and preferred timing so we can prepare for the walkthrough.

Can drywall repair be included before painting?

Yes. Painting-related patches, surface preparation, priming and painting can be listed in the proposal. Plumbing, electrical and unrestricted handyman work are not offered.

What affects exterior scheduling?

Surface moisture, rain, temperature, drying conditions and the preparation required can affect the schedule. The written proposal identifies the included surfaces and coating steps.
