Start with the condition of the wood

A useful estimate begins with the surface—not only its square footage. We inspect weathering, peeling or failing coatings, mildew, exposed wood, previous stain or paint, moisture concerns and areas that may need repair before refinishing. The written scope identifies the surfaces, preparation and finish included before scheduling.

Preparation for a lasting exterior finish

Depending on the condition and selected coating, preparation may include protecting nearby areas, cleaning, scraping loose material, sanding rough or failing sections, spot priming compatible surfaces and allowing adequate drying time. Surface washing is offered only when it is part of the staining or exterior-painting preparation; R Painters LLC does not advertise standalone pressure washing.

Stain, solid-color finish or paint

The existing coating and desired appearance affect the available finish. Transparent and semi-transparent stains allow more wood character to show, while solid-color stains and compatible paint systems provide greater color coverage. We review the existing surface and project goals before documenting a recommendation; a new coating cannot correct active rot, structural movement or unresolved moisture.

Decks, fences, porches and rails

Qualifying projects can include deck boards, stairs, risers, rail components, fences, gates, porch floors and related exterior wood. Each estimate defines exactly which faces and components are included so there is no uncertainty about hard-to-reach or non-visible areas.

How to request a clearer estimate

Share the property location, current photos, approximate dimensions, the surfaces you want finished, the existing coating if known, and your preferred color or level of wood visibility. We may recommend an on-site walkthrough to confirm access, preparation and material needs.
