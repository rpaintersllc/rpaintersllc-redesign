Interior painting prep: what Charleston homeowners should plan before painting

Practical guidance from R Painters LLC for Charleston and Lowcountry property owners.

The finish coat gets most of the attention, but the preparation before painting often determines how clean and professional the final result looks. A well-planned interior project should account for repairs, protection, sheen, trim and access before the first wall is painted.

Start with the condition of the walls

Small nail holes, drywall patches, settlement cracks, stains and previous touch-ups can become more noticeable after fresh paint is applied. During a walkthrough, identify which defects should be repaired and whether any stained areas need a stain-blocking primer before finish paint.

Decide exactly what is included

“Paint the room” can mean different things. Clarify whether the project includes walls only or also ceilings, baseboards, crown molding, doors, door frames, closets, built-ins and accent walls. A clear scope helps avoid surprises and makes estimates easier to compare.

Choose sheen with the room in mind

Sheen affects both appearance and cleanability. Flat finishes can help minimize surface imperfections, while eggshell or satin finishes are often selected where additional washability is desired. Trim and doors are commonly finished in a more durable enamel with a higher sheen.

Protect floors and furnishings

Furniture should be moved or grouped away from the walls, while flooring and remaining items should be protected before sanding, repairs or painting begins. Good protection also makes the project easier to keep organized from room to room.

Think about color transitions

When changing from a dark color to a light color—or from a strong accent color to a soft neutral—the coating plan may require additional coverage or primer. Color changes on ceilings, trim and built-ins should also be considered separately from wall color.

Prepare for the walkthrough

Before an estimate, it helps to make a short list of rooms and surfaces, note known drywall repairs and decide whether you want help with color selection. Photos can also help us prepare before the appointment.
