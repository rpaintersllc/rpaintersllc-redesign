# BUZZIE lead operating model — competitor research, October 28, 2026

## Decision

Build a painting-specific local acquisition and qualification service first. Keep the homeowner workspace as a conversion and follow-up tool. The operating loop is traffic → consented request → contact confirmation → project qualification → one-company assignment → estimate → outcome. This is a proposed operating model, not a claim that new qualification, attribution, contact-preference or reminder features are active.

Keep the existing private pilot closed to the public. No competitor signup, fake homeowner inquiry, sales contact, advertising purchase, billing, SMS or public-access change was performed for this research.

## Observations from primary sources

| Service | Published method | Method worth adapting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Painter Choice / PaintingLeads.com | Painting-specific homeowner acquisition; project/contact information and timing; prompt contractor notification; valid-lead charging and bad-contact credit rules. Its public homeowner page offers three quotes; another published FAQ describes distribution to 1–4 contractors. | Painting specialization, concise project intake, measurable contact quality, rapid handoff and documented invalid-lead reasons. |
| Networx | Its help center describes search/marketing/content/affiliate acquisition, shared distribution to up to four contractors and an exclusive option that attempts verification calls/live transfers during business hours. | Clear source tracking, explicit shared/exclusive rules and a human verification step. |
| Thumbtack | Official community guidance describes contractor lead-price and weekly-budget controls tied to job priorities. | Service/location/capacity preferences and transparent limits before dispatch or future charging. |
| Houzz | Distinguishes project questionnaires/matching, direct messages, calls and website-origin leads. | Painting-specific scope questions and separate source categories rather than combining all activity into one lead total. |
| 99 Calls | Markets exclusive inbound leads using contractor websites, organic search and advertising, with follow-up/CRM tools. | Own the acquisition funnel and measure each channel through actual estimates and completed outcomes. |

These are published operating descriptions and vendor claims, not independently verified conversion performance. Painter Choice pages show varying pricing descriptions and older materials; its current Charleston/Summerville commercial offer, exclusivity and refunds require a written market-specific quote if ever purchased. Do not copy advertised success percentages or promised job counts into BUZZIE.

## Homeowner proposition

Prepare the project before the first sales conversation; request a free estimate without booking work or committing to hire. State clearly that a participating painting company receives the request and will identify itself when contacting the homeowner. During this pilot only R Painters participates. Preserve operator transparency in privacy information; never suggest several painters competed or were compared when they were not.

One-company assignment means BUZZIE does not distribute the request to competing contractors. It does not mean the homeowner is prohibited from requesting quotes elsewhere or has never contacted another company.

## Proposed qualification workflow

1. **Request received:** save service, city/ZIP, scope, timing and consent. Offer photos without forcing them. No payment or compulsory account to start.
2. **Contact preference:** collect preferred channel and contact window. Explain what contact follows submission. This preference capture is a next feature, not currently deployed.
3. **Reachability confirmed:** record an actual two-way reply or verified channel. Email confirmation, telephone verification, consent and project readiness are separate facts. Turnstile does not verify the person's phone, identity or intention to buy.
4. **Scope confirmed:** establish an actual painting-related project, authority to arrange the work, covered location and intended timing. Keep exploratory planning separate from near-term requests. A customer seeking estimates is a legitimate prospect, not automatically a bad lead.
5. **Assigned:** one active participating business whose coverage and capacity fit. Record assignment and time; do not silently sell or transfer a rejected request elsewhere.
6. **Accepted and followed up:** operator sees time received, contact state, source, scope and age. Start with a staffed response target during published business hours, not an unsupported 24/7 promise. Reminders should respect preference, consent, quiet hours and a small attempt limit.
7. **Estimate and outcome:** requested → reachable → qualified → estimate scheduled → estimate completed → quote → won/lost. Record loss reasons. A lead, an appointment and a sold job are different results.

For R Painters, prioritize interior/exterior painting, cabinet refinishing and painting-related drywall repairs. Standalone pressure-washing requests should not be treated as an eligible painting lead. Small legitimate work can remain a separate bucket instead of being labelled fake or silently discarded.

Initial invalid-request reasons: nonexistent/wrong contact, duplicate request, outside agreed coverage, unrelated service, no authority to arrange work, or confirmed spam. A customer choosing another contractor, declining a price or taking longer to decide is a sales outcome, not by itself proof of a fake lead.

## Acquisition focus

Begin with distinct Summerville and Charleston painting funnels, plus service-specific entry points for interior, exterior and cabinets. Record source/medium/campaign and landing page through the request and outcome. Do not create numerous cloned city pages or imply physical offices or contractor availability that do not exist.

Use genuine work examples with permission, permitted community/referral placements and, only after separate spending approval and launch readiness, a capped acquisition experiment. Do not divert the existing R Painters website/SEO program automatically or claim that a private owner-only page can already acquire public homeowner traffic.

Google Search Ads and Google Local Services Ads are different products. Google's Local Services policy prohibits lead selling/passing leads to other businesses and requires accurate provider branding. Do not use a BUZZIE reseller identity to acquire and redistribute LSA leads. Any eligible R Painters LSA activity remains directly branded/fulfilled by R Painters, separate from BUZZIE marketplace acquisition.

## Prioritized implementation backlog

1. Close remaining native Auth, branded SMTP/confirmation/recovery and mobile gates.
2. Add contact channel/window and carry it into the contractor workspace.
3. Add structured qualification and loss reasons with an audit trail; keep phone/channel verification distinct from anti-bot success.
4. Add a source-to-outcome report and timestamps for first response/estimate. Exclude every system-test record from customer/conversion metrics.
5. Add restrained, consent-aware follow-up controls after delivery and staffing are proven.
6. Validate with a small cohort of genuine authorized homeowners. Review abandonment and failed contacts before increasing traffic.
7. Defer contractor billing, nationwide activation and additional-company administration until the local funnel is repeatable.

Existing verified pieces include request intake, bot checks on intake, one-company routing, private photos, account linking, two-way workspace messages, visit decisions, private estimate tracking and won/lost statuses. Important gaps include branded external email, account CAPTCHA activation, real mobile acceptance, structured contact preferences/qualification and demonstrated repeatable customer acquisition. Workspace message/visit updates do not currently send email/SMS.

## Commercial model after validation

R Painters is the internal pilot operator, not an external paying contractor. Initially judge BUZZIE by real qualified requests, completed estimates, won jobs and profit after acquisition and delivery costs.

If outside contractors are later added, test a transparent capped plan with a defined valid-lead allowance and explicit invalid-lead credits; evaluate whether unused allowance rolls over or is refunded before charging anyone. Do not promise a fixed monthly number until supply and acquisition cost support it. Contractor service fees and advertising spend must be separately disclosed.

Track visitor-to-request rate, reachable/qualified share, response time, completed-estimate rate, win rate, acquisition cost per qualified request, acquisition cost per won job and contribution after job costs. Small-cohort results are exploratory, not forecasts. Public-post discovery stays a separate prospect source and never counts as a consented BUZZIE homeowner request.
