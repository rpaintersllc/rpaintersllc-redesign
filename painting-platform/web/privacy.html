This page explains how the current Buzzie pilot handles your information.

Who operates the pilot

Buzzie — by R Painters — connects homeowners with a local painting company. The current pilot serves the Charleston, South Carolina area. No nationwide contractor network is active.

What you share

A project request includes your name, email, phone, city, ZIP code, selected service, project description, property type, timing and optional budget range and photos. Your contact permission is recorded. Basic request-source tags and a hashed network identifier help attribution and limit repeated submissions.

Who can access your project

Your assigned business can view your request and photos through its authenticated workspace. You can view them using your private project link. Buzzie routes your request to the next available local painting company serving your area. Requests are not distributed to multiple providers or automatically sent to prospects by the discovery tool.

Protect your private link

Anyone with the complete project link can view the project and photos. Keep it private. The access key is in the link fragment and is not included in the server page URL. If you choose Remember on this device, your browser stores the project ID and access key locally. Anyone using that browser may then open your saved projects. Clearing browser storage removes the saved link from that browser, but does not delete the project itself.

Storage and notifications

Projects and private photos are stored in the operator’s Supabase project. Photos use temporary signed viewing URLs rather than a public gallery. Generic email alerts to the operator use Resend and omit your project contact details. The app is hosted with ChatGPT Sites. Confirmation is shown on the project page; homeowner email verification and automatic homeowner email updates are not active in this pilot.

Requests and contact

Contact details are supplied by the requester and are not independently verified. Submitting is not a booking, a contract for painting work or an agreement on price. Discuss scope, scheduling and terms directly with the painting company.

Corrections and removal

Contact Buzzie support or 843-475-9927 for help with access, corrections or removal requests. These requests are handled manually during the pilot. Do not post your private link publicly.

Business interest

Painting businesses may submit their business name, email, website and location for expansion discussions. This does not activate a provider account, create a subscription or guarantee any leads.
