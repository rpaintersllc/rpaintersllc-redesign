# Homeowner painting platform — local pilot

The product is an owner-controlled marketplace platform, with a homeowner project
journey and a business workspace. The discovery monitor is a separate acquisition
channel, not the product itself.

Pilot identity: Painting Projects, by R Painters. This is a working product name,
not a claim that a national brand or domain has been secured.

Homeowners can choose a service, check local coverage, submit project scope and
contact permission, upload private photos, retain a private project access link,
and track status. The active provider is R Painters LLC. Do not imply a network
of vetted contractors, guaranteed quotes, verified contacts, invented reviews,
or nationwide availability.

Business memberships scope project access. Future provider activation can extend
the same provider/region records, but automated onboarding, identity screening,
billing, nationwide matching and transactional homeowner email delivery are not
completed by this pilot. Guest access uses a private, high-entropy project key;
it is not an email-verified homeowner account.

Launch gate: test the full homeowner and provider journeys, finalize public brand
and domain, verify production bot protection and transactional email sender, and
approve public audience before promotion. The initial Site remains owner-private
for this testing. No production website/main merge is included.
